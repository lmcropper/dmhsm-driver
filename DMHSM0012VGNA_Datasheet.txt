1.3 General Specification

The DMHSM0012VGNA silicon-based micro-display employs a fully digital driving solution, supporting 8-bit
parallel data input. It is manufactured using a 0.18-micron CMOS process. The display's orientation, position, and
built-in testing functions can be controlled and adjusted through a 2-wire serial programming interface. The interface
voltage standard is 1.8V CMOS, making it suitable for a wide range of miniaturized, high-resolution near-eye display
systems.

5.0 Reliability

Cleaning: It is recommended to use a clean cloth or lens tissue for wiping. Do not use any corrosive

chemicals for cleaning.

Electrostatic Protection: The internal chips of the product are made using CMOS technology, which is
sensitive to static electricity. It is recommended to use the product in areas away from charged objects or areas
with anti-static equipment. Operators should wear appropriate anti-static clothing.

Precautions:It is recommended to use protective gloves when handling the product.
Do not expose the product to environments with corrosive chemicals such as acids or alkalis.

Do not submerge the product in liquid.
Avoid contact with sharp objects and impacts from external forces.
Storage Method: The product may have a shortened lifespan or be damaged if exposed to excessively high
or low temperatures. The recommended storage environment is:
Room temperature: approximately 25°C, fluctuating by +5°C.
Environment: A dry, sealed container in a stationary state to avoid impacts.
